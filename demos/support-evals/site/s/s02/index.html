How do I export all my invoices as a CSV?

CustomerHow do I export all my invoices as a CSV?
AssistantGo to Billing → Invoices, select All, then choose Export → CSV. The file is emailed to you within a few minutes.
Policy Invoices can be exported from Billing → Invoices → Export (CSV or PDF zip). Large exports are emailed.
