Retry failed card payments

About 3.1% of renewals fail on the first charge, most with soft declines (insufficient funds, issuer unavailable). Today we give up at once and email the customer, and 41% of them churn.

What we'll build

  1. Retry soft declines on a schedule: after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days, at the hour the card last succeeded.
  2. Never retry hard declines (stolen card, closed account); email the customer straight away as now.
  3. Keep the subscription active through the retry window, with a banner asking to update the card.

Rollout

Week 1Retry worker behind a flag, 5% of renewals
Week 250%, compare recovery against the control group
Week 3Everyone, if recovery is above 25%

Risks

  • Retrying too often can get us flagged by the card networks: cap at 4 attempts in 30 days.
  • Customers charged on day 7 may not expect it: say the date in the first email.
