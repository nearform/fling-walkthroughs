PAY-431ApprovedPlan by Ana Lopez

Refunds to the original payment method

Support issues refunds as account credit because the refund screen only knows about credit. 18% of refund tickets come back asking for the money instead.

What we'll build

  1. Add 'Refund to card' to the support refund screen, using the payment provider's refund call.
  2. Partial refunds by line item, with a reason that appears on the credit note.
  3. Keep account credit as an option, no longer the default.

Rollout

Week 1Provider refunds behind a flag for the support leads
Week 2All of support

Risks

  • Cards that have expired since the payment: the provider routes the refund; we show 'may take 10 days'.
